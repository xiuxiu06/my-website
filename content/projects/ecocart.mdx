<TLDR>

A Chrome extension built in 24 hours at MakeUC 2025 by a team of four. It reads the items in your Amazon cart and recommends alternatives that are both affordable and more sustainable, without leaving the page. It won **Second Place**, **Best Green Tech Hack** and **MLH Best Use of Gemini API**. I built the extension's frontend and the scraper: I designed the UI and captured product data from shopping sites to feed into the AI, and helped with the backend when needed.

</TLDR>

## The idea

Shopping more sustainably usually means extra research at exactly the moment you're ready to check out. EcoCart brings that research to the cart: it looks at what you're about to buy and puts greener, still affordable options right next to it.

## What we built

- **A cart reader.** The extension captures the products in your cart, the data the rest of the pipeline works from.
- **Product classification and sustainability scoring** for each item.
- **Recommendations** from a pipeline that combines product classification, Gemini analysis and vector similarity search.
