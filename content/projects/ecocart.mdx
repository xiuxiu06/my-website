<TLDR>

A Chrome extension built in 24 hours at MakeUC 2025 that recommends cheaper, greener alternatives for what's in your cart. It won **Second Place**, **Best Green Tech Hack** and **MLH Best Use of Gemini API**. I built the extension's UI and the scraper that feeds product data to the AI.

</TLDR>

## Highlights

- Reads the products in your Amazon cart, right where you check out.
- Classifies each item and scores how sustainable it is.
- Recommends alternatives using Gemini analysis and vector similarity search.
