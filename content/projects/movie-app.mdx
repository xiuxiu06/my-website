<Overview>

A solo project: a movie discovery app on the TMDB API. Search is debounced, results scroll endlessly, and a trending list ranks movies by what people actually search for, stored in Appwrite.

</Overview>

## Highlights

- Real-time search, debounced by 500 ms so typing doesn't flood the API.
- Infinite scroll for endless browsing.
- A live top 10: every search updates a count in Appwrite, and the most searched movies rise.
- An animated background, a floating hero and a glowing custom cursor, built with GSAP.
