<TLDR>

A clinical interview simulator for healthcare students, built in 24 hours at MakeUC 2026 and awarded **MLH Best Use of Gemini API**. I built the trust engine, the interface, the RAG debrief, login and deployment.

</TLDR>

## Highlights

- Interview an AI psychiatric patient by voice (Gemini Live) or text.
- A **trust engine** scores every turn and decides what the patient will reveal.
- **Rewind** to a key moment, ask a better question and compare the two runs.
- A scored debrief that cites clinical guidelines (C-SSRS, SAFE-T, PHQ-9, OARS) through RAG.
