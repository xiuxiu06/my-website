[Why practicing clinical interviews, especially suicide risk assessment, is hard for students: few chances to practice, high stakes, little feedback on *which* question changed the outcome.]

## What we built

- **Voice or text interviews** with a live AI patient (Gemini Live), with lip-synced avatars.
- **A trust engine.** Every turn is classified (empathy, reflection, dismissiveness, direct suicide inquiry…). Trust from 0 to 100 controls which facts the patient will reveal and how they sound.
- **Checkpoints and rewind.** Key moments are saved, so you can replay from one and compare two runs side by side.
- **A scored debrief** out of 100, with feedback that cites C-SSRS, SAFE-T, PHQ-9 and OARS through retrieval.
- **Classroom roles.** Students and instructors log in with Auth0; instructors publish cases to the class.
