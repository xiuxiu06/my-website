## The problem

Most of us wear a small part of our closet over and over while the rest waits on the rail. Wardrobe apps try to fix this, but they start by asking you to photograph and log everything, and most people give up after a week.

## What we built

- **Hangers that track wear.** A few minutes off the rail means you were browsing; hours away means you wore it. A small hub in the closet sends these moments to the app.
- **Three small lights per hanger.** Blue for today's outfit, a slow yellow breathe for pieces that have waited a while, two white blinks for "here I am" when you search. No red or green: nothing says your clothes are "bad".
- **An app that plans with you:** today's outfit for the weather, a week of plans, and a swipe deck of forgotten pieces to keep, style or let go.
- **A closet camera** that photographs a garment as it's hung back, removes the background and names it, so photos happen without effort.
