<TLDR>

Smart hangers that notice what you actually wear, with no photos or logging needed. Built by a team of three for Innovation Challenge 2026 (top 25% of projects). I built most of the web app and the hardware, my first hardware project taken all the way to a finished product.

</TLDR>

## Highlights

- Each hanger knows when a garment leaves and comes back: minutes away means browsed, hours means worn.
- A small light on every hanger marks today's outfit, forgotten pieces, and the item you searched for.
- The app plans outfits for the weather and suggests pieces to keep, restyle or let go.
- Hangers run on an ESP32-C3 with two switches, an LED and a LiPo battery.
