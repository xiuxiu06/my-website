<Overview>

A recipe-sharing social app built as a team at RevolutionUC 2025 and named after the hackathon's mascot, Leo. It's written in Python with Streamlit, with SQLite for storage and a chatbot for cooking questions.

</Overview>

## Highlights

- Create a profile, browse a meal feed, and like or save recipes.
- Share your own recipes with photos.
- Ask the built-in chatbot for help in the kitchen.
- Seven pages, each built separately by the team and then merged into one app.
